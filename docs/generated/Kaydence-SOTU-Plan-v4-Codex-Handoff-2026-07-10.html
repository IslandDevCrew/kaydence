ARCHIPELAGO — Jon's evidence-gated build methodology

A full-cycle build protocol: typed contracts at every seam, gates that must be able to fail, loopback routing, and a tamper-evident ledger. The one rule: nothing crosses a gate on claims alone — a claim is not done until a captured, hashed piece of evidence proves it. A falsified claim caps the audit at band 1; an unverified claim at band 3; a UI claim with no runtime evidence at band 4.
