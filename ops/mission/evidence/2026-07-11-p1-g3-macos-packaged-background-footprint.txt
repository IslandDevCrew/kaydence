MEASUREMENT CONTRACT
- Tool: /usr/bin/footprint --noCategories --format bytes.
- Visible set: kaydence + launch-adjacent WebKit GPU, Networking, and WebContent.
- Background set: the same PID plus only its surviving launch-adjacent WebKit
  services. Stale/unrelated WebKit services are excluded by before/after PID.
- Charter budget: every no-model idle state < 80 MB (80,000,000 bytes) and
  idle CPU < 1%. Visible and background states are evaluated separately; a
  background pass does not close a visible-state failure.
- App-data models directory contained no model artifact. The selected q5 engine
  therefore remained Blocked rather than loading a model.

ROOT CAUSE
- The historical 85,376 KB result was process RSS, not a physical-footprint
  process-group measurement. The later ~86 MB status combined a 30 MB main
  physical footprint with an estimated WebKit group and was not reproducible.
- Fresh exact-byte measurement shows WebContent is the controlling visible-only
  allocation. A separately built no-ASR control measured 86,527,336 bytes
  visible, the same range as production, so Whisper linkage is not the cause.
- Hide-only retains WebContent and does not solve the no-model visible budget.
  Destroying the closed cockpit solves the always-on background cost only.

IMPLEMENTATION
- The tray uses the existing checked-in application icon and APP_NAME.
- Open focuses an existing window or recreates `main` from tauri.conf.json.
- Window-close is intercepted only long enough to destroy the WebView and mark
  the resulting last-window exit request as background keep-alive.
- Programmatic Quit and ordinary Command-Q are not intercepted.
- No dependency, network call, model byte, telemetry, or persistence surface was
  added.
