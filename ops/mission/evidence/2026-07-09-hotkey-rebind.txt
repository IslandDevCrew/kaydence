Slice: backend-owned recommended hotkey binding rebind and live desktop re-registration

What changed
- Added an allowlisted primary hotkey binding setting with recommended options: RightAlt, F13, F14, Control+Space, and Shift+F13.
- Added set_hotkey_binding Tauri command. Rust normalizes and validates the binding, persists it to app-data settings.json, and returns the updated AppSnapshot.
- Changed the global-shortcut handler to consult the managed active shortcut instead of closing over the startup shortcut, so later rebinds are observed by the runtime.
- Rebind registers the new shortcut before unregistering the old one, refuses while capture is active, and leaves an old working binding active when a new candidate fails.
- Startup now retains the HotkeyRuntime even if the initial default shortcut registration fails, so a one-tap rebind can recover the same runtime without restarting.
- The cockpit renders backend-provided binding options in Output rules and shows a compact first-run recovery strip when hotkey registration failed.

Safety and boundaries
- The frontend renders binding options supplied by AppSnapshot and sends only a binding intent; it does not parse arbitrary key input or own shortcut semantics.
- This does not claim live OS permission or conflict proof across macOS, Windows, and Linux. The local evidence proves parser/persistence/runtime wiring and browser-rendered recovery UI.
- No network, telemetry, new dependency, or new OS permission surface was added.
