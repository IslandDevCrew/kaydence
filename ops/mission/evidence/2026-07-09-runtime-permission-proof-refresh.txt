Behavior
- settings::sync_first_run_permission_requirements updates only runtime-proven rows:
  - microphone -> Ready after capture produced persisted local audio.
  - macOS input_monitoring -> Ready after the selected hotkey reached Kaydence from the OS event stream.
- The hotkey runtime records microphone proof only when finalized capture audio has samples_written > 0.
- Zero-sample WAL-only captures do not mark microphone proof ready.
- Tail-tick completion proof can be drained after the release handler via refresh_first_run_runtime_proofs.
- The first-run setup panel has a compact Refresh action beside Free selected. In Vite preview it refuses native IPC and shows the desktop-runtime boundary note.
