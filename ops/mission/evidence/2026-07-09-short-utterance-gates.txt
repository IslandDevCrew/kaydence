  actually executes the kill/recover suite instead of passing with 0 tests.
- Added apps/desktop/src-tauri/tests/short_utterance.rs.
- The new short_utterance suite covers the currently implemented P1 portion of
  Pitfall P1:
  - 0.3s push-to-talk capture is accepted and finalizes only after the 300ms tail.
  - 1.5s toggle capture is accepted and finalizes only after the 300ms tail.
  - <250ms accidental tap is discarded and its WAL file is removed.
  - 0.3s and 1.5s short WAL clips recover without truncation after a no-finalize/crash shape.
