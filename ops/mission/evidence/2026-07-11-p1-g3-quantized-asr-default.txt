ROOT-CAUSE INVESTIGATION
- Canonical f16 Metal report failed only resident RAM: 268.188 MB, p50/p95
  54/77 ms, idle CPU 0%.
- Five and ten f16 samples both settled around 270 MB, so this was first-use
  residency rather than per-dictation accumulation.
- macOS vmmap showed roughly 80 MB of resident empty malloc regions after
  inference-state teardown. Flash attention was tested as one variable and
  reverted: 271.813 MB, p95 116 ms. Explicit allocator pressure relief was
  tested as one variable and reverted: 268.547 MB, p95 254 ms.
- The model itself was the controlling variable. Native logs report 147.37 MB
  Metal model size for f16 versus 59.12 MB for Q5_1.
