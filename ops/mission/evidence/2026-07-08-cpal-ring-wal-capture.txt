Scope:
- Added cpal 0.17.x and ringbuf 0.5 as local, non-network audio dependencies.
  cpal resolves to 0.17.3 under the repo's Rust 1.80 baseline.
- Production WalCaptureRuntime::new now prepares a default cpal input stream on
  StartCapture. The stream is not played until after the WAL header is created.
- The realtime input callback only downmixes interleaved PCM frames to f32 mono
  and pushes into a lock-free SPSC ring buffer with try_push. It does not lock,
  allocate, log, write files, or call into ASR.
- A drain thread owns the WalWriter and appends ring-buffer samples to disk.
  FinalizeCapture stops the stream, joins the drain, patches/fsyncs the WAV
  header, and reports dropped input sample count.
- Tests use WalCaptureRuntime::new_wal_only so CI never depends on microphone
  permission, while production HotkeyRuntime::new uses the default mic path.
