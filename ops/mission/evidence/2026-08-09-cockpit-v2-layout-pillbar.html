Treatment: a slim horizontal pill bar, sized to Windows Voice Typing's (Win+H) toolbar proportions, but made genuinely dockable to a screen edge — with the internal layout reflowing to vertical when docked left/right, the way Wispr Flow's Flow Bar does. Shows mic state, a live timer, a tiny waveform, and a single-word Whisper-Ahead preview inline — all four states you'd otherwise only get in the full Cockpit.
