Kaydence runtime history wiring slice
Date: 2026-07-08
Commit: pending at evidence capture

Intent
- Wire the new SQLite HistoryStore into the hotkey runtime.
- Persist finalized WAL audio metadata immediately after capture finalization.
- Keep discarded short taps out of history.
- Surface history write failures through the runtime error path instead of
  silently losing the safety-net record.

Implementation summary
- HotkeyRuntime now owns a HistoryStore alongside the WalCaptureRuntime.
- HotkeyRuntime::new opens history.sqlite3 under the app-data directory.
- HotkeyRuntime::new_wal_only opens the same store in tests.
- FinalizeCapture records the CaptureSessionSummary::audio_persisted_event into
  history before logging the persisted event.
- Added HotkeyRuntimeError to carry both audio and history failures.
- Extended runtime tests:
  - finalized WAL creates history.sqlite3;
  - history session records the WAL path and one AudioPersisted event;
  - discarded short-tap WAL leaves no history session.
